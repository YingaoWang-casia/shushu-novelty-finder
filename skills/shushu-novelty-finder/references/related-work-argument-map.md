# Related Work Argument Map

A paper-ready idea needs an argument map, not just a paper list.

## Argument Map Template

```text
Line 1: Task definition papers
What they established:
What they did not cover:
How our idea differs:

Line 2: Method papers
What they improved:
Shared assumptions:
Remaining weakness:
How our idea differs:

Line 3: Benchmark or dataset papers
What they measure:
What they miss:
How our idea changes evaluation:

Line 4: System or deployment papers
What constraints they consider:
What constraints they ignore:
How our idea exposes the gap:

Line 5: Analysis or survey papers
What they conclude:
What open questions remain:
How our idea answers one question:
```

## Required output

For every medium or strong idea, produce:

```text
Closest paper cluster:
Key papers:
What prior work already solved:
What prior work did not solve:
Our exact gap:
Why this is not just an incremental variant:
```

## Writing rule

Do not write related work as a chronological list. Write it as a set of competing research lines that create the gap.

## Reviewer check

If the gap cannot be expressed as a contrast against at least two paper clusters, the idea is probably not mature enough for a strong paper.
